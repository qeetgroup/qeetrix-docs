## Examples

### Default

Give the avatar a `name` and the fallback shows the initials, announced as the name; without one it shows a neutral person icon. Sizes are fixed steps from `xs` (20px) to `xl` (48px), and `shape="square"` marks an organisation or app rather than a person.

<ComponentPreview name="avatar/1-default" />

### Group

`AvatarGroup` overlaps its avatars and rings each one off from the next. Name every avatar, and give the overflow count a full sentence with `aria-label`.

<ComponentPreview name="avatar/2-group" />

### With badge

`AvatarBadge` puts a mark on the avatar's bottom-end edge. Colour alone is not a status: here the row says it in words, so the avatar is hidden from screen readers; with no visible text, put the status inside the badge as `sr-only` text.

<ComponentPreview name="avatar/3-with-badge" />
