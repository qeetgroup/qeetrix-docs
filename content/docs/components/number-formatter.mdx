## Examples

### Default

A display-only wrapper over `Intl.NumberFormat`. Pass a `locale` (`en-IN` groups by lakh and crore) and any other format option through `options`; `decimals` fixes the fraction digits. Tabular numerals keep a column of figures aligned.

<ComponentPreview name="number-formatter/1-default" />

### Compact

`notation="compact"` shortens large figures for tiles and charts, in the locale's own units: crore and lakh in `en-IN`, millions in `en-US`. `prefix` and `suffix` add text around the number.

<ComponentPreview name="number-formatter/2-compact" />
