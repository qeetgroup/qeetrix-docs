
It can also be imported on its own from `@qeetrix/ui/components/alert-dialog`.

### Controlled and uncontrolled

## Accessibility

Follows the WAI-ARIA `alertdialog` pattern. Not yet audited.

### Keyboard

<kbd>Tab</kbd> <kbd>Shift+tab</kbd> <kbd>Escape</kbd>

### Focus

Each control is a Tab stop, in order. While open, focus is contained and focus returns on close.

### Known limitations

- **Focus:** Same `inert` limitation as Dialog.
