## Examples

### Default

A link for each level above the page, then the page itself as `BreadcrumbPage`, which is marked `aria-current="page"`. `BreadcrumbSeparator` draws a chevron and is hidden from assistive technology.

<ComponentPreview name="breadcrumb/1-default" />

### Collapsed

A long path keeps its first and last levels and folds the middle into `BreadcrumbEllipsis`. Wrapped in a menu trigger, it reveals the hidden levels; its visually hidden "More" text names the trigger for screen readers.

<ComponentPreview name="breadcrumb/2-collapsed" />

### Custom separator

Children of `BreadcrumbSeparator` replace the chevron. An icon-only crumb, like the home link here, needs an `aria-label`.

<ComponentPreview name="breadcrumb/3-custom-separator" />
