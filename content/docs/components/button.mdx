## Examples

### Variants

One primary action per view; secondary and outline beside it, ghost in toolbars, and destructive for actions that can't be undone.

<ComponentPreview name="button/1-variants" />

### Sizes

Four text sizes, and icon-only sizes that need an `aria-label`.

<ComponentPreview name="button/2-sizes" />

### Loading

`loading` shows a spinner, sets `aria-busy` and ignores clicks while keeping focus. `loadingLabel` replaces the label for the duration and holds the button's width.

<ComponentPreview name="button/3-loading" />
