## Examples

### Default

One panel open at a time: opening another question closes the first. `defaultValue` takes an array of item values, here opening the first answer on load.

### Multiple

`multiple` lets several panels stay open side by side, for a reviewer comparing policy sections. An item can also be `disabled` on its own, such as a feature the plan doesn't include.
