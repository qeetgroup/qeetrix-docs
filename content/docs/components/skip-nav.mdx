## Examples

### Default

The skip link stays out of view until it has focus: click the line above the frame, then press Tab. Enter jumps past the header to `SkipNavContent`, the page's `<main>`, so the next Tab lands on its first control. In an app it comes first in `<body>` and is fixed to the viewport; here the frame's transform contains it.
