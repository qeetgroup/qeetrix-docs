## Examples

### Default

Click a pill to add or take back your reaction, or pick a new one from the add-reaction picker. Your own reactions take the selected style, and hovering or focusing a pill shows who reacted.

<ComponentPreview name="reaction-bar/1-default" />

### Read only

`readOnly` shows the reactions without letting the viewer change them: no picker, and the pills aren't buttons. Counts of 1,000 and up are shown compact.

<ComponentPreview name="reaction-bar/2-read-only" />
