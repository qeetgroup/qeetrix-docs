## Examples

### Default

Right-clicking the trigger area, or long-pressing it on touch, opens the menu at the pointer. The items are Dropdown Menu's: icons, shortcuts, separators and a destructive variant.

<ComponentPreview name="context-menu/1-default" />

### Checkbox items

Checkbox items toggle a setting and leave the menu open. `ContextMenuLabel` names a group and has to sit inside a `ContextMenuGroup` or `ContextMenuRadioGroup`. Here a log viewer offers copy and filter actions, plus view options.

<ComponentPreview name="context-menu/2-checkbox-items" />

## Accessibility

Follows the WAI-ARIA `menu` pattern. Not yet audited.

### Keyboard

<kbd>Enter</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> <kbd>Escape</kbd> <kbd>ArrowUp</kbd> <kbd>ArrowDown</kbd> <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd>

### Focus

One Tab stop for the group; arrow keys move between items (roving tab stop). While open, focus is contained and focus returns on close.
