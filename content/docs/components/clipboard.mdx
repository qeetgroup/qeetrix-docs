## Examples

### Copy button

`CopyButton` copies its `value` and confirms in place. It only shows "Copied!" once the browser has confirmed the write, and keeps its width so nothing beside it moves.

<ComponentPreview name="clipboard/1-copy-button" />

### Use copy to clipboard

`useCopyToClipboard` is the hook behind `CopyButton`, for a trigger of your own. `copy` resolves `true` once the write is confirmed, and `copied` stays true for the timeout.

<ComponentPreview name="clipboard/2-use-copy-to-clipboard" />
