## Accessibility

Follows the WAI-ARIA `combobox` pattern. Not yet audited.

**Keyboard:** <kbd>Tab</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> <kbd>Escape</kbd> <kbd>ArrowUp</kbd> <kbd>ArrowDown</kbd> <kbd>Home</kbd> <kbd>End</kbd>

**Focus:** Focus stays on the input; arrow keys move the active option (`aria-activedescendant`).
