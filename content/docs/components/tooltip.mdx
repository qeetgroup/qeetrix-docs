## Examples

### Default

A short label for a control, shown on hover and on keyboard focus. Touch devices don't show tooltips, so the icon-only button still names itself with `aria-label`.

<ComponentPreview name="tooltip/1-default" />

### Group

`TooltipProvider` gives a group one shared delay: once a tooltip is open its neighbours open instantly, so sweeping along a toolbar doesn't wait at every button. A `Kbd` in the content shows the control's shortcut.
