## Examples

### Default

Steps before `activeStep` show as complete, the active step is marked `aria-current="step"`, and later steps are upcoming. Drive `activeStep` from your own wizard state; at the number of steps, every step is complete.

<ComponentPreview name="stepper/1-default" />

### Vertical

`orientation="vertical"` stacks the steps, for side panels and narrow flows. `invalid` draws a step in the destructive treatment with "!" and announces "Has errors"; put the reason in its `description`.

<ComponentPreview name="stepper/2-vertical" />
