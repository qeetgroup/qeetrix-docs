## Examples

### Default

A panel anchored to its trigger, for a quick setting that doesn't need a dialog. It isn't modal: Escape or a click outside closes it, and focus goes back to the trigger.

<ComponentPreview name="popover/1-default" />

### Placement

`side` picks the edge of the trigger the panel opens from, and `align` lines it up along that edge. A trigger at the end of a header opens below with `align="end"`, so the panel stays under the header instead of hanging past it.

<ComponentPreview name="popover/2-placement" />
